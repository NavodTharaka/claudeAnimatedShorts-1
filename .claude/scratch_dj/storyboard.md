# Storyboard: Where Does Your Food Go After The Stomach?

Takeaway: food only becomes part of you once it is broken into pieces small enough to absorb. Style: Deep Dive 3D (same as the breath-hold short), three worlds: the real person, the X-ray body, inside the gut. Target ~95 s, 9:16.
Reuses the series person from "What Happens Inside Your Body When You Eat" so it feels like the same show; the shots are new.

Notes
- No on-screen text beyond the burned-in karaoke captions. All labels are shapes and colours (tubes, beads, glows).
- No gore or toilet humour: the waste is a plain brown-grey, shown schematically, never graphic.
- New shot about every 3-4 s (22 shots, ~95 s, so some shots run 4-5 s with a camera move). Shots 2/3 and 7/8 repeat a door motif on purpose: big chunk fails, small bead passes.
- Voice: edge-tts en-US-AndrewNeural -2% (as in the breath-hold short). Music: warm score, ducked under voice, kept below voice and effects.
